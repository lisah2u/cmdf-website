Our Programs

Discover how we transform lives through music education, community dance, and special events

What We Offer

Our programs provide comprehensive music education, community engagement, and cultural preservation opportunities for children, youths, families, and community members.

Scholarships

The Dakota Karper Music Scholarship program provides 30 weeks of private music instruction, group band classes, and instrument access for students in grades 3rd-10th.

Community Square Dances

Our square dances welcome dancers of all skill levels and ages. No experience or partner needed - just come ready to dance and have fun!

Special Events

Join us for workshops, field trips, and special community events that bring together musicians, dancers, and families throughout the year.
