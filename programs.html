Ages 6-10 | Sprouts Program

Sprouts of Music

Our youngest musicians begin their musical journey with fundamental music education, rhythm training, and introduction to various instruments in a fun, supportive environment designed specifically for early childhood development.

✓

Music Fundamentals

Basic rhythm, melody, and music theory through play and exploration

✓

Instrument Exploration

Hands-on experience with percussion, brass, and woodwind instruments

✓

Social Development

Building confidence, teamwork, and communication skills

✓

Academic Support

Reading and math reinforcement through music-based activities

Ages 11-17 | Flagship Program

Marching Crusaders

Our premier marching band program combines traditional New Orleans brass band music with modern marching techniques, discipline, and performance excellence. Students develop advanced musical skills while building character and leadership abilities.

✓

Performance Excellence

Advanced ensemble performance and competition preparation

✓

Leadership Development

Section leader roles and peer mentorship opportunities

✓

College Preparation

Academic tutoring and college application assistance

✓

Community Engagement

Performance opportunities at local festivals and events

Requirements

  • • Minimum 2.5 GPA maintained
  • • Regular attendance at rehearsals and performances
  • • Commitment to program values and behavior standards
  • • Participation in community service activities
All Ages | Innovation Program

Roots Studio Academy

Advanced music production, recording techniques, and contemporary music education preparing students for careers in the modern music industry. Our state-of-the-art studio facility provides hands-on experience with professional equipment and software.

🎛️ Music Production

Pro Tools, Logic Pro, and industry-standard software training

🎤 Recording Arts

Professional recording techniques and audio engineering

💼 Music Business

Industry knowledge, marketing, and entrepreneurship

🎵 Songwriting

Creative composition and collaborative songwriting workshops
