Our mission

🎵
Empower children and youth via access to high-quality music education, break down aspirational and social barriers to music education, cultivate a diverse community of passionate, young musicians, and foster creativity, discipline and self-confidence.
🎻
Celebrate the rich cultural heritage of our community through the promotion of and education participatory folk dance with the intention of nurturing social unity, preserving cultural identity, and providing transformative experiences to individuals across multiple generations.

What We Do

We support comprehensive music education and provide help to young people and our community to reach their full potential through the transformative power of music.

🎵

Music Education

Professional instruction in folk instruments, building technical skills and musical knowledge for children and youth.

🤝

Square Dances

Community square dances featuring local callers and bands bringing together neighbors for music and dance.
