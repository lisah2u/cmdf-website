Our mission

🎵
Foster an environment where children and youth can access music education, with priority given to students with financial needs.
🎻
Perpetuate folk life traditions and expand Community via instruction in multi-generational folk dance.
🪕
Inspire a lifelong love for music and dance while nurturing the next generation of talented artists and leaders who will enrich our world with their unique voices and contributions.

What We Do

We provide comprehensive music education and support to help young people and our community reach their full potential through the transformative power of music.

🎵

Music Education

Professional instruction in folk instruments, building technical skills and musical knowledge for children and youth.

🪕

Ensemble Sessions

Community jam sessions and group performances that connect students with local musicians.

🤝

Square Dances

Community square dances featuring local callers and bands bringing together neighbors for music and dance.

Get Involved

Apply for a youth scholarship or join a square dance! We welcome your support and involvement in our programs whether you are a student, parent, or community member.
