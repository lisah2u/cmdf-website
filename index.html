Creating the Next Generation of New Orleans Musicians

Founded in 2007 by Grammy winner Derrick Tabb (CNN Hero), The Roots of Music strengthens communities by transforming the lives of children through music education, academic support, and community engagement. Our mission focuses on youth empowerment for low-income households, serving children ages 3-18 in New Orleans.

What We Do

We provide comprehensive music education and support to help young people reach their full potential through the transformative power of music.

🎵

Music Education

Professional music instruction in various instruments and ensemble performance, building technical skills and musical knowledge.

📚

Academic Support

Tutoring and homework assistance to ensure our students excel in school while pursuing their musical passions.

🤝

Community Engagement

Performance opportunities and community events that connect our students with the broader New Orleans community.

Our Programs

Discover our comprehensive programs designed to nurture musical talent and support academic achievement.

Ages 9-18

Core Music Education

Comprehensive music education including history, theory, instrumental instruction, and ensemble performance. Students receive professional-level training in traditional New Orleans music and modern techniques.

Instrumental Instruction
Ensemble Performance
Music Theory & History
Academic Support
K-1st Grade

Sprouts of Music

Early childhood music program in partnership with KIPP Believe School, introducing our youngest students to the joy of music through age-appropriate activities and after-school sessions.

Program Features:

  • • KIPP Believe Partnership
  • • After-school Sessions
  • • Age-appropriate Activities
  • • Foundation Building
Ages 15-18

Roots Studio Academy

Advanced program focusing on audio engineering, studio production training, and music business education. Students gain hands-on experience with professional equipment and industry knowledge.
