## 1. FAQ Section

> "Q&A format about classes, locations, and how to join. LLMs often scrape FAQ content to answer queries directly."

FAQ content currently exists across **three separate pages**. A consolidated FAQ page or section would strengthen SEO. Below is what exists and what's missing.

### Existing: General Program FAQs (from contact.html)

**Q: How do I enroll my child in a program?**
A: You can start the enrollment process by filling out our online application form or contacting our enrollment coordinator at (304) 555-ARTS. We'll schedule a meeting to determine the best program fit for your child.

**Q: Are there any costs for participation?**
A: Our programs are offered at low cost to ensure accessibility. Scholarships are available for those who need financial assistance. This is made possible through the generous support of our donors and community partners.

**Q: What if my child has no musical or dance experience?**
A: No prior musical or dance experience is required! Our programs are designed to welcome students at all skill levels. We provide comprehensive instruction starting from the very basics.

**Q: How can I support the Cacapon Music and Dance Foundation?**
A: There are many ways to support our mission including financial donations, volunteering, instrument donations, and corporate partnerships. Contact us to learn more about how you can get involved.

### Existing: Donation FAQs (from donate.html)

**Q: Where does my donation go?**
A: Your sponsorship sustains the heartbeat of our community by providing instruments, lessons, kid jams, youth mentorship, community square dances, and year-round educational programming. Every dollar creates opportunities for learning, connection, and celebration in Hampshire County.

**Q: Is my donation tax deductible?**
A: The Cacapon Music and Dance Foundation is a tax-exempt nonprofit organization recognized under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law.

**Q: Will I know who I'm sponsoring?**
A: Gold and Platinum sponsors are matched with a child and receive a simple annual update celebrating their progress. We respect every family's privacy and always communicate with care.

**Q: Can I sponsor anonymously?**
A: Absolutely. Please simply email to let us know that you would like your donation to be anonymous.

**Q: Can I make a one-time donation instead?**
A: Yes. If sponsorship isn't right for you now, one-time gifts of any amount still make a meaningful difference. If you'd prefer your contribution pays for a square dance, please let us know and we'll match you with a dance!

**Q: Do donations stay local?**
A: Yes. Every sponsorship supports children and families right here in our community.

**Q: What if I want to sponsor as a family, group, or business?**
A: Wonderful! Shared or corporate sponsorships are welcome. Contact us and we'll make it easy.

### Existing: Square Dance FAQs (from dance.html)

**Q: Do I need to know how to dance?**
A: No! Our square dance welcomes dancers of all skill levels and all age groups. No experience is necessary to dance at a square dance. Prior to the start of each dance, the 'Caller' walks everyone through the steps of how to dance the next set, and then as the dance is going on, the caller continues to sing/call out directions for the dancers to follow along.

**Q: Do I need to bring a partner?**
A: Though in the dance you are often paired up with a partner, you don't need to bring one in order to dance. There are always lots of individuals in attendance looking for a partner to get out on the dance floor with. Come alone or bring a friend to this community event.

**Q: What should I wear?**
A: There is no required outfits at our square dance. Wear an outfit and shoes that you can move in, are comfortable and make you feel good!

**Q: What should I bring?**
A: You are likely to get hot dancing, so be sure to bring a water bottle so that you can stay hydrated. Also consider stopping by one of Capon Bridge's locally-owned restaurants to pick up food or beverages.

**Q: When are the dances held?**
A: Check out our schedule and upcoming dates on the dance page, or visit our events calendar for the latest updates.
