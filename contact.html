Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our programs and enrollment process.

How do I enroll my child in a program?

You can start the enrollment process by filling out our online application form or contacting our enrollment coordinator at (304) 555-ARTS. We'll schedule a meeting to determine the best program fit for your child.

Are there any costs for participation?

Our programs are offered at low cost to ensure accessibility. Scholarships are available for those who need financial assistance. This is made possible through the generous support of our donors and community partners.

What if my child has no musical or dance experience?

No prior musical or dance experience is required! Our programs are designed to welcome students at all skill levels. We provide comprehensive instruction starting from the very basics.

How can I support the Cacapon Music and Dance Foundation?

There are many ways to support our mission including financial donations, volunteering, instrument donations, and corporate partnerships. Contact us to learn more about how you can get involved.
