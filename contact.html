Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our programs and enrollment process.

How do I enroll my child in a program?

You can start the enrollment process by filling out our online application form or contacting our enrollment coordinator at (504) 555-JOIN. We'll schedule an interview and audition to determine the best program fit for your child.

Are there any costs for participation?

All of our programs are completely free, including instruments, materials, meals, and transportation. This is made possible through the generous support of our donors and community partners.

What if my child has no musical experience?

No prior musical experience is required! Our programs are designed to welcome students at all skill levels. We provide comprehensive instruction starting from the very basics.

How can I support The Roots of Music?

There are many ways to support our mission including financial donations, volunteering, instrument donations, and corporate partnerships. Contact us to learn more about how you can get involved.
