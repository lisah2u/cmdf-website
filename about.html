About Us

Learn about our mission, history, and impact on the Capon Bridge, WV community through music and dance education.

Our Mission

The Cacapon Music and Dance Foundation strengthens communities by transforming the lives of children through music and dance education, academic support, and community engagement. We believe that every child deserves access to quality arts education and the opportunities it provides for personal growth and cultural preservation.

Through our comprehensive programs, we work to preserve West Virginia's rich musical heritage while building the next generation of musicians, dancers, and community leaders in Capon Bridge and surrounding areas.

Our Story

From humble beginnings to a cornerstone of the West Virginia arts education community.

2007

The Beginning

Founded to preserve West Virginia's rich cultural heritage, the Cacapon Music and Dance Foundation was established to provide hope and opportunity to children in rural West Virginia through the transformative power of music and dance. We started with just a handful of students and a vision to celebrate our Appalachian traditions while building community connections.

2012-2018

Growth & Expansion

As our impact became clear, we expanded our programs to include traditional Appalachian dance, academic support, mentorship, and community engagement. We developed our signature youth programs and established partnerships with local schools and community organizations throughout Hampshire County.

2019-Present

Innovation & Impact

Today, we serve students of all ages and have become a cornerstone of arts education in rural West Virginia. Our programs combine traditional Appalachian music and dance instruction with modern techniques, ensuring our cultural heritage continues to thrive while preparing students for contemporary opportunities.
