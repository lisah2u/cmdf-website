About Us

Learn about our mission, history, and impact on the New Orleans community through music education.

Our Mission

The Roots of Music strengthens communities by transforming the lives of children through music education, academic support, and community engagement. We believe that every child deserves access to quality music education and the opportunities it provides for personal growth and community connection.

Through our comprehensive programs, we work to break cycles of poverty and violence while building the next generation of New Orleans musicians and community leaders.

Our Story

From humble beginnings to a cornerstone of the New Orleans music education community.

2008

The Beginning

Founded in the wake of Hurricane Katrina, The Roots of Music was established to provide hope and opportunity to children in New Orleans through the transformative power of music. We started with just a handful of students and a vision to rebuild our community through music.

2012-2018

Growth & Expansion

As our impact became clear, we expanded our programs to include academic support, mentorship, and community engagement. We developed our signature Marching Crusaders program and established partnerships with local schools and community organizations.

2019-Present

Innovation & Impact

Today, we serve over 150 students annually and have become a model for music education programs nationwide. Our Roots Studio Academy represents the cutting edge of music education, combining traditional instruction with modern technology and career preparation.
