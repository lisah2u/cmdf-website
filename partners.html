Our Partners

Our mission to enrich lives through music and dance is made possible through the generous support of our partners and sponsors. These organizations, businesses, and individuals share our commitment to fostering musical education, preserving cultural traditions, and building stronger communities.

From financial sponsors who fund scholarships and programs, to local businesses that support our events, to those of you that add to the dance donations jar -- each partnership plays a vital role in creating opportunities for children and families in Hampshire County and beyond. Together, we're building a vibrant musical community where everyone can participate, learn, and grow.
