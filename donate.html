Who We Serve

Your support directly impacts children and families in our community who need access to music education and cultural experiences.

Children in Need

Children from low-income families who cannot afford music lessons or instruments receive scholarships and access to our programs.

Home-Schooled Children

Home-schooled children who lack access to group music education and ensemble opportunities find community and musical growth through our programs.

Cacapon Music and Dance Foundation is a registered nonprofit organization dedicated to ensuring all children in Hampshire County can experience the benefits, joy, and confidence that music brings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does my donation go?

Your sponsorship supports instruments, lessons, kid jams, youth mentorship, and year-round educational programming. Every dollar directly benefits children in Hampshire County.

Is my donation tax deductible?

Yes. The Cacapon Music and Dance Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. (Insert official wording + EIN here once finalized.)

Will I know who I'm sponsoring?

Silver, Gold, and Platinum sponsors are matched with a child and receive a simple annual update celebrating their progress. We respect every family's privacy and always communicate with care.

Can I sponsor anonymously?

Absolutely. You can request anonymity at checkout.

Can I make a one-time donation instead?

Yes. If sponsorship isn't right for you now, one-time gifts of any amount still make a meaningful difference. If you'd prefer your contribution pays for a square dance, please let us know and we'll match you with a dance!

Do donations stay local?

Yes. Every sponsorship supports children and families right here in our community.

What if I want to sponsor as a family, group, or business?

Wonderful! Shared or corporate sponsorships are welcome. Contact us and we'll make it easy.
